# Faberrgén

*Fab / err / gén*

```
fab = fabricate
err = make errors
gen = generate
```

[Faberrgén](https://faberrgen.com/) is an anti-capitalist, anti-communist, **community-focused** & most importantly **anti-egoist** incubator for creative technology startups and other social(ist)/populist/profitable/or not ideas™️.

## What brings a community together?

Faberrgén seeks to facilitate activities that bring people/minds/ideas together, both as live and remote events encouraging participation/hands-on creation, sharing, re-use, jaw drops, lightbulb moments.

Activities and events help to program the physical space and virtual space(s) of the incubator and include popular/common/proven forms of interaction and natural growth:

* discussions
* "friend therapy"
* workshops
* workouts
* walkabouts
* meditation
* reading groups
* film screenings
* performances
* art shows
* community gatherings

## What's important about the project

* Faberrgén is not a typical SV-style incubator with a grind and make it big mindset
* work/life/play/vacay balance is of utmost importance to the Faberrgén WoL (way-of-life)
* Faberrgén strives to be a force for good within individual/family/world/community
