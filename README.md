#### Elevator pitch

[Faberrgén](https://faberrgen.com/) is a **community-focused**, anti-capitalist, anti-communist & most importantly **anti-egoist** incubator for creative technology startups and other social(ist)/populist/profitable/or not ideas™️.  

#### Who are we?

The founders of Faberrgén are the same folks who have been running the design & technology prototyping studio [Aeolab](https://aeolab.com/) the last **20+ years**.  Our philosophy has always been to stay **small** and **scrappy** and we've seen and worked with many small and big fish that have come and gone in the giant pond that is the city of Los Angeles over the years.  We are **LA-local** first, **California-vibes** second, **global presence** third.

#### What are we building?

With technology accelerated to the present moment, we believe **agentic-first** lean companies (**1-2 founders**) are the future of new ideas and vitality in business, particularly with new companies that embrace a **community spirit** and a strong **aesthetic sense** informed by **bespoke craftsmanship** of the underlying technologies, their design language(s) and community action.

#### What makes a community?

Faberrgén seeks to **facilitate activities** that bring people/minds/ideas together, both as **live and remote events** encouraging participation/hands-on **creation, sharing, re-use,** jaw drops, lightbulb moments. 🧠 (((💡))) 🧠

Activities and events help to **program the physical space and virtual space(s)** of the incubator and include popular/common/proven forms of interaction and **natural growth**:
* discussions
* workshops
* walkabouts
* reading groups
* film screenings
* performances
* art/design/tech shows/demos
* community gatherings

#### How do we help you as a new lean startup?

Our offices in Los Angeles provide you with a **small but cozy environment** to grow your embryo and hopefully hatch a golden egg at the end of a **5-month process** 🏭🥚⚙️🐣.

We provide you with ready-to-go **physical space** and **set up business/legal infrastructure** common to technology startups in the West Coast scene. We furnish **technical/philosophical/business/legal support** to guide you through the process of very initial growth from a 1-2 person idea team to a baby chick of a new fangled company, ready to take wade into the big pond and take on the world 🌊🐥⛵.

#### What's important to us about the project

* Faberrgén is not a typical SV-style incubator with a grind and make it big mindset 
* work/life/play/vacay balance is of outmost importance to Faberrgén WoL (way-of-life)
* Faberrgén strives to be a force for good within individual/family/world/community
* the remaining/paying cohort support all activities of Faberrgén per cycle
* Faberrgén aims to break even in early stages, make 120 - 200% profit eventually
* (nice to have) one Faberrgén incubatee per cohort/cycle as free/sponsored
